Who it is for

A food diary that starts where you are.

For adults in Singapore who want a clearer view of what they eat—whether the focus is everyday calories or closer limits for sugar, salt and fat.

01

For everyday awareness

Set a calorie goal, record meals and see the day or month at a glance. Simple enough to keep using after the first week.

02

For closer nutrition limits

Turn on a mode that watches sugar, salt and fat, with limits you can adjust to match guidance from your doctor or dietitian.

What it does

Ten useful tools. One calmer routine.

From setting a goal to choosing a meal within budget, every feature supports a practical decision you make around food.

01

Set a calorie goal

Get a suggested daily target from a few details, then adjust it to suit you.

02

See your diary and reports

Track meals through the day and look back across the month at your calorie figures, shown as estimates.

03

Find meals that fit today

Filter suggestions by your diet choice and allergies, and always check the food label.

04

Open a suggested meal's recipe

When a meal appears in Suggestions, open its recipe from an online recipe service.

05

Save your own recipes

Keep homemade favourites with their ingredients, servings and calories per serving in one place.

06

Log any meal

Add hawker dishes, your recipes or a calorie amount you already know.

07

See one weekly nutrient tip

After at least four completed days in the past week, see the nutrient you fell furthest short of and one food tip.

08

Stay in control of your data

Protect your account, download your information or delete everything inside the app.

09

Watch sugar, salt and fat

Set daily limits and see a warning before a meal takes the day over one of them.

10

Keep an eye on food spending

Set a daily food budget and get suggestions that fit your calories and money left, with estimated prices or “No price”.

Calorie targets, health limits and nutrient notes are guidance only, not medical advice or a diagnosis.

Privacy, made plain

Your food story stays yours.

Sign-up asks only for an email and password. NutriKaki does not keep your name.

  • ✓Details that make it useful. The app asks for body details to suggest a calorie goal, then keeps them in your account with your food diary and any settings you choose.
  • ✓Download your data. Take a copy from inside the app whenever you want.
  • ✓Delete from the app. Remove your account and its data without sending an email.
